Approved evidence pack

Separate signals from conclusions.

Each source contributes something different. Your job is to identify what the combined evidence supports—and what it still cannot prove.

01 · Funnel export

Eight weeks, 42,801 sessions, channel and device cuts.

Look for patterns, but do not treat one data cut as causation.✓ Approved source
02 · Support themes

318 tickets tagged by onboarding complaint.

Use as corroboration; check selection, sample, and denominator.✓ Approved source
03 · Campaign notes

Paid-social mix changed during week five.

Use for timing and context; correlation still requires a test.✓ Approved source
Analysis sequence

What should you do?

  1. Define the outcome precisely.
  2. Find the largest credible change.
  3. Compare evidence across sources.
  4. Name the uncertainty.
  5. Choose the cheapest decisive test.

Why the exchange works

Small companies get work without adding payroll. Students get paid experience without waiting for permission.

Important tasks often sit unfinished because a small team cannot justify a full-time hire, internship program, or agency retainer. At the same time, undergraduates need credible work that fits around class. ProofPath turns that mismatch into one bounded, fixed-price exchange.

For small companies

Buy one useful outcome at the level you can afford.

Get help on a two-to-twelve-hour project without creating a job or managing an open-ended freelancer engagement.

  • Set the deliverable, fixed budget, deadline, data boundary, and acceptance rule first
  • Match to a student with relevant simulations and accepted work
  • Review in under 30 minutes, then rebook strong contributors
For undergraduates

Turn open hours into money, judgment, and proof.

Complete real, compensated work around class, keep the upside of working efficiently, and turn each accepted result into portable evidence.

  • Know the pay, scope, deadline, and data boundary first
  • Build experience without waiting for a formal internship
  • Discover which work is energizing before committing to a career
Fair-work rule: ProofPath is not a cheap-labor marketplace. Useful company work is paid; faster completion never reduces the agreed student fee; unpaid assessments must use synthetic or de-identified simulations; and every launch market requires employment, tax, privacy, and worker-classification review.
