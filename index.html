    What Covenda is

    Proof of real work,
    not another résumé line.

    Covenda turns a company’s delayed, low-risk work into a scoped, reviewed project—so students earn paid experience and keep evidence employers can trust.

    One profile. Evidence tied to the work.

    Covenda does not reduce you to one universal score. Each project can show the specific capabilities, inputs, review, revisions, and outcome behind it.

    The best first project is useful without being risky.

    Start where context can be made portable, the finish can be reviewed, and the student does not need access to critical production systems.

    Good first fitPublic-source research, data cleanup, QA, documentation, workflow mapping
    Needs redesignUnclear deliverables, heavy live supervision, undocumented context
    Not eligibleProduction access, client or patient records, regulated decisions, restricted information

    A pipeline of proof, not another job board.

    Students on your roster get bounded, reviewed, paid projects and keep evidence tied to real work. You stay in the loop without running a marketplace or managing accounts.

    Good fitCareer centers, departments, clubs, and student orgs sharing interested students
    How data is usedNames and school emails go to the Covenda pilot team only—no public listing
    Not includedAccounts, rankings, automatic placement, or a guarantee of paid work
