    Where the scoring stands. V1 is a transparent weighted model: it reads the ideal intern you describe against what a student has evidenced, and it shows which parts matched, which did not, and how many were compared at all. The machine-learning re-ranker is built and running in shadow, deliberately not influencing any score until there are enough completed outcomes to train it honestly, because a model fitted on a handful of results is worse than the arithmetic it would replace.       How proof works

      Machines check facts.
      Experts judge work.

      Not a trade-off. A machine proves the account and history are yours; a person judges whether the thinking was any good. We run both.

      Machine-checked · objective

      Facts that hold up without anyone's opinion: who owns the account, when the work accumulated, whether the formulas compute.

        Expert-vetted · judgement

        What no API can answer: can they defend the decisions they made, and will someone who supervised them stake their name on it.

          Two raters score a walkthrough against published anchors and adjudicate where they disagree, judgement, measured. What never counts as proof is a simulation or a self-reported number.

          Why now

          The thing you can’t put on a résumé
          is the thing that now matters most.

          Building something used to take capital, a team, and years. It takes a fraction of that today, which moves the scarce thing from credentials to judgement. Taste, agency, knowing which problem is worth solving. None of it fits in a bullet point, and all of it shows up in an afternoon of real work.

          The cost of building collapsed

          What needed a team and a year now needs a weekend and good judgement. That is why a startup will hand real work to a nineteen-year-old who can show they have done it.

          Credentials price the wrong thing

          A degree certifies you cleared an admissions bar at seventeen. It says nothing about whether you can scope an ambiguous problem, the only skill that has gone up in value.

          Being early looks like being wrong

          The work worth doing is usually the work most people dismiss. Nobody hands you a credential for conviction. You get it by shipping something and being right.

          Proximity is still the whole game

          Careers move through people who have seen your work. That is why Covenda runs on vouching, the network you need is built by being useful to people, early, before you need anything.
