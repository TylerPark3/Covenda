Why this exists

Hiring broke on both ends.

If you hire

Tired of AI-written applications?

Every application now reads well. Hundreds arrive, polished by the same models, and none of them tell you who can actually do the work. Screening got slower, not faster.

What Covenda does: shows you the work first — a connected account, a parsed model, a recorded walkthrough — evidence with a source you can open.

If you're a student

Tired of being underestimated?

You get filtered on graduation year, on age, on whether anyone recognizes your school — decided before a single person looks at what you've built.

What Covenda does: scores evidence, not background. School prestige, name, and age are not inputs — and a test fails the build if any of them moves a score.

      Why now

      Companies underestimate
      what an undergraduate can do.

      Even the ones who know better have no reliable way to find the students who will actually deliver. That is the real gap — not capability, but discovery.

      • 52%of recent graduates are underemployed in their first job — sorted into roles that never required their degree, and never tested beyond them.Burning Glass Institute & Strada, 2024
      • 45%are still underemployed a decade later. The initial misread compounds; it does not quietly correct itself.Burning Glass Institute & Strada, 2024
      • 7%New graduates are now just 7% of Big Tech’s hires — entry-level hiring there has more than halved since 2019. The front door most students were told to use is closing.SignalFire State of Talent, 2025
      • 50%of students say they would rather build their own business than climb a corporate ladder. The ambition to do real work is already there — startups are where it belongs, if the two can find each other.Morning Consult for Samsung Solve for Tomorrow, 2023

      These figures cite published research, not Covenda results. Covenda has no outcome data yet — the pilot is how we earn it.

      Every ambitious student hits the same wall: “I could contribute here — I just can’t get in front of the founder.” Another mass application won’t carry them; AI made that pile too deep. What still works is what always worked: someone credible stakes their name on you, you get one real shot, and the work proves the rest.

      Why Covenda

      The problem isn’t talent.
      It’s noise.

      AI made mass applications free — so everyone sends thousands, and the students who can actually deliver disappear into the pile. Covenda is the opposite bet: a short, vouched, reviewed list. Signal, not spam.

      The open pile

      • Thousands of look-alike applications, most of them AI-written
      • Founders and HR sift résumés no one wrote by hand
      • The students who’d actually deliver are invisible in the volume
      • Everyone competes on quantity — a race no one wins

      Covenda

      • A short list, vouched by a professor, club, or industry pro
      • Proven on a reviewed work-trial before it reaches you
      • Curated and selective on purpose; the vetting is the value
      • Judged on what they can do, not just where they studied
