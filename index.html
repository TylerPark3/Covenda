What belongs here

Useful work that can be bounded, reviewed, and paid.

ProofPath starts with the business problem, not a generic job post. We help isolate an external-safe work unit and define what “done” means before a student begins.

01 · Shape

Clear output

A workflow map, research brief, QA log, organized dataset, checklist, or another reviewable deliverable.

02 · Boundary

Safe inputs

Public, synthetic, redacted, or explicitly de-identified information. No production credentials or regulated judgment.

03 · Decision

Named reviewer

One person can inspect the output against visible acceptance criteria without recreating the whole project.

Good early fit

  • Deferred work with a visible output
  • Five to fifteen hours of student effort
  • Public, synthetic, redacted, or de-identified inputs
  • A reviewer who can make one clear decision

Not suitable

  • Tax, investment, medical, legal, or compliance conclusions
  • Client records, client documents, or production credentials
  • Open-ended staff augmentation with no acceptance rule
  • Work where oversight is likely to exceed the time saved
Current stage · honest by design

Founder-assisted pilot intake—not an open marketplace.

We are testing whether a small number of accounting teams will pay for a well-scoped project, accept the output, and report that it saved senior time. Student interest is real intake; projects and jobs are not guaranteed.

About ProofPath

Built around the people who do the work and the teams that must review it.

ProofPath is an independent, founder-led project. We are building a managed bridge between capable students who need credible paid work and small teams carrying important work they have not been able to prioritize.

We are starting narrowly in accounting operations, learning manually, and treating accepted paid projects—not sign-ups or polished screens—as the evidence that matters.

Independence: ProofPath is not an official Columbia University program and does not claim university, employer, or company partnerships.
