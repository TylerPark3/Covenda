# Covenda GTM — Accounting / Finance-Ops Wedge

The go-to-market **is** the product demo: pre-scoped packets, arriving via a vouched
intro, pay-on-acceptance. If that motion won't convert where you have real network
depth, that's decisive evidence — which is the point.

**How to use this file:** everything below is a template or a tracker. You (the
founder) fill in the `[bracketed]` bits, approve every send, and send it yourself.
Nothing here is auto-sent. **Do not ship any outcome number until it's real** — the
case study is blank until the first batch produces data.

---

## The go / no-go tracker (fill this in before you start, update per batch)

Decide the verdict in advance so a bad result reads as *evidence*, not failure. These
are judged **by the end of the second batch**:

| Signal | Threshold | Batch 1 | Batch 2 | Reading if missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paying firms | **≥ 5** | ___ | ___ | Demand isn't there at this price/motion |
| Sourced via **referral** vs. cold | **Majority referral** | ___ | ___ | If cold wins, the "vouched intro" thesis is wrong |
| Review burden per project | **≤ [X] min** (set your ceiling) | ___ | ___ | Margins don't hold; delivery is too raw |
| Repeat purchase within 2 cycles | **> ~50%** | ___ | ___ | Value isn't sticky → willingness-to-pay is thin |

Pick the review-burden ceiling now (suggest **90 min/project**). If cold outperforms
referral, or repeat rate lands near zero, that's a **model signal to pivot** — and you
know it after two batches, not a year.

## Artifact 1 — Pre-scoped Project Packet

You walk in with this already built from the firm's **public** info. Their job shrinks
from "figure out what to delegate" to **yes / no**. One packet per prospect.

### Template
```
PROJECT PACKET — [Firm name]
Prepared by Covenda · [date]

Outcome:        [the useful thing they get, in one line]
Deliverable:    [the exact artifact — file/format]
Boundary:       [what's explicitly IN scope, and what's OUT — no scope creep]
Inputs needed:  [public sources only, or the one thing they'd hand over]
Acceptance:     [the 2–4 criteria that mean "done and correct" — they approve these]
Reviewer:       [who on their side signs off — a named person, ~[X] min]
Price:          [N] credits ($[N]) · pay only on acceptance
Timeline:       [N] business days from go
```

### A starter library for this wedge
Reusable packet skeletons for the common, low-risk, seasonal backlog. Adjust price to
"clearly beats an hour of their staff's time." (Credits: 1 credit = $1.)

**A. Competitor fee benchmarking** *(illustrative — e.g. a 15-person RIA)*
- Outcome: know how your fee schedule sits against 8 comparable local/peer firms.
- Deliverable: a cited spreadsheet — each competitor's published tiers + positioning notes, one summary tab with where you're high/low.
- Boundary: public sources only (websites, ADV filings, Form CRS); no outreach to competitors; no fee recommendation (facts, not advice).
- Acceptance: 8 firms covered · every number linked to its source · summary tab flags the 3 biggest gaps.
- Reviewer: partner/advisor, ~30 min. Price: **400 credits** · 10 business days.

**B. Reconciliation / cleanup pass** *(post-tax-season, Apr–Jun)*
- Outcome: a clean, categorized month you were behind on.
- Deliverable: reconciled ledger export + a list of flagged/uncertain items for your call.
- Boundary: one entity, one period; categorize + flag only — no adjusting entries posted; you approve anything ambiguous.
- Acceptance: every transaction categorized or flagged · flagged items have a one-line reason · ties to the bank statement.
- Reviewer: bookkeeper/controller, ~45 min. Price: **300–500 credits** by volume · 7–10 days.

**C. Close-prep checklist + working papers** *(pre-year-end, Oct–Nov)*
- Outcome: walk into close with the prep already done.
- Deliverable: a close checklist tailored to your entity + a populated working-papers folder (schedules, tie-outs from the docs you provide).
- Boundary: prep and organization only — no opinions, no filings.
- Acceptance: checklist covers your recurring close items · each schedule ties to a source · gaps listed explicitly.
- Reviewer: controller, ~60 min. Price: **500–700 credits** · 10–12 days.

**D. Process documentation** *(any time; low risk, high relief)*
- Outcome: a repeatable, written version of a process that lives in one person's head.
- Deliverable: a step-by-step SOP with screenshots + a one-page quick-reference.
- Boundary: one named process; document current state (not redesign).
- Acceptance: someone new could follow it unaided · every step has the who/what/where · edge cases noted.
- Reviewer: process owner, ~30 min. Price: **250–400 credits** · 7 days.

## Cadence — how to actually run it

1. **Pick the season.** If it's Apr–Jun, lead with reconciliation/cleanup; if Oct–Nov, close-prep. Otherwise process docs (always-on) + benchmarking.
2. **Work the partners first** (Artifact 3). Aim for a handful of warm employer intros before any cold outreach — that's the thesis test.
3. **Pre-scope a packet** (Artifact 1) for each warm intro from public info.
4. **Send the intro** (Artifact 2, warm) leading with pay-on-acceptance.
5. **Announce the batch** (Artifact 4) to create the deadline.
6. **Instrument every project** (Artifact 5 metrics) so batch 1 manufactures the sales asset.
7. **Update the tracker** after each batch and read the verdict honestly.

**~80% of your GTM time goes to the employer side.** Supply isn't the bottleneck.
