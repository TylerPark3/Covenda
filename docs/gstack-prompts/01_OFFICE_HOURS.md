# OFFICE HOURS

> Source: Covenda GStack Prompt Pack. Paste into the matching GStack skill.

We are defining the narrowest credible first version of Covenda.

Read `docs/COVENDA_GSTACK_CONTEXT.md` and the following parts of `docs/COVENDA_MASTER_SPEC.md` before questioning me:

- Executive system map
- Chapters 1–4
- Chapter 29: Marketplace Dynamics and Cold Start
- Chapter 30: Go-to-Market
- Chapter 34: Implementation Roadmap and Stage Gates
- Chapter 43: Decision Ledger

Also inspect the current repository and browse `https://covenda.app` so the conversation is grounded in what already exists.

Run this as YC-style startup office hours. Challenge the company, not merely the feature list.

Force clarity on:

1. The exact first user and first desperate problem.
2. Why a technically strong student would build a Covenda profile before many companies participate.
3. Why a 5–75-person technical startup would use Covenda instead of referrals, LinkedIn, Wellfound, Handshake, or direct outreach.
4. The narrowest transaction we can complete manually with 25–50 students and 5–10 companies.
5. Which existing app behavior should be preserved, removed, or reframed.
6. Whether our first wedge is discovery, evidence verification, shortlisting, Company DNA, paid trials, or a deliberate combination.
7. What we can observe in 30, 60, and 90 days that would invalidate the idea.
8. The smallest product that produces a real introduction, evaluation, or hire.
9. Which parts of the 400-page vision are strategic infrastructure versus premature complexity.
10. How Covenda can deliver immediate student value without pretending a marketplace already has liquidity.

Non-negotiables:

- Do not collapse Covenda into a project marketplace.
- Paid trials are optional.
- Referrals are optional.
- Do not propose a universal student score.
- Do not treat school prestige as capability.
- Do not expand all batches or verticals in V1.
- The existing app is the implementation baseline, not disposable scaffolding.

End by writing a concise product design document with:

- problem;
- first user;
- wedge;
- core transaction;
- status quo;
- why now;
- 90-day pilot;
- explicit cuts;
- falsifiable assumptions;
- success and kill criteria;
- recommended next GStack command.
