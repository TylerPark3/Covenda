# DESIGN CONSULTATION

> Source: Covenda GStack Prompt Pack. Paste into the matching GStack skill.

Create or refine Covenda's product design system without discarding the existing brand.

Read `docs/COVENDA_GSTACK_CONTEXT.md`, Chapter 41 of the master spec, the current UI, and the live site.

The desired product feeling is:

- credible;
- evidence-rich;
- intellectually serious;
- warm rather than bureaucratic;
- closer to a living professional body of work than LinkedIn;
- closer to an investigative evidence interface than a generic dashboard;
- appropriate for students, founders, professors, and hiring managers.

Develop:

- design principles;
- typography;
- spacing;
- color roles;
- evidence status language;
- verification status;
- uncertainty presentation;
- graphs and timelines;
- cards and tables;
- forms;
- consent and privacy patterns;
- empty and error states;
- mobile;
- accessibility;
- motion rules;
- realistic mockups for the student profile and company shortlist.

Avoid decorative prestige, résumé templates, generic gradients, unexplained score rings, and visual ranking of people.
