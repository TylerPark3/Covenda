# Covenda — Positioning & Website Master Prompt

Use this to translate Covenda's thesis into website copy and features. It captures
**what Covenda is really doing**, the narrative for each audience, and the concrete
site changes that express it. Every external statistic is listed in the **Grounding
checklist** at the end — none of it ships until it's verified against a real source.

---

## ROLE & GOAL
Make the marketing site say, in the founder's own thesis: *the internship/early-talent
market is broken by oversaturation and AI-spam; Covenda is the curated, referral-vouched
alternative that surfaces a small number of genuinely capable students to the companies
that need them.* Land this for **two audiences** (students, companies) without ever
sounding like "a second Handshake."

## THE ONE-LINE THESIS
**Covenda is a curated talent layer — professor- and club-vouched, high-achieving
students matched to real work — built for a world where AI has made mass applications
worthless and raw student capability higher than ever.**

## THE CORE NARRATIVE (why now)
Four forces, in order:

1. **Demand is real and rising.** Hundreds of thousands of high-capacity undergraduates
   want professional experience in their field. Hiring them is hard, time-intensive, and
   high-stakes to get right — and there are *more applicants per role than ever*, with
   acceptance rates falling. *(Grounding: applicant-volume + acceptance-rate trend.)*

2. **The old system is broken by design.** Internships carry long-seated biases —
   employers fear the time/effort of training someone who then returns to being a
   student or leaves (even to a competitor). *(Grounding: intern → full-time conversion /
   "return to company" rate — the ~60% figure needs a real source.)*

3. **AI flipped the equation twice.** (a) Student *capability* is up — with AI tools in
   every student's hands, a motivated undergraduate can contribute real work far beyond
   what the old "needs hand-holding" assumption allowed. (b) Company *behavior* is
   shifting — heavy spend on AI agents/tools, volatile software-hiring (layoffs that are
   already rebounding as "AI automates everything" proves false), and traditional intern
   sourcing (Handshake et al.) drowning in **AI-generated spam applications** that make it
   expensive and slow for HR/startups to find the right person. *(Grounding: enterprise
   AI/agent spend; SWE layoff-then-rebound; skills-based hiring shift.)*

4. **The proven workaround is private — Covenda universalizes it.** The people who hire
   the best early talent already skip the platforms and go through **trusted referrals**
   (professors, clubs). (Anecdote: the Rootic startup operator sources this way, not via
   job boards.) Covenda turns that private, relationship-driven pipeline into a platform.

## POSITIONING — "the anti-Handshake, on purpose"
- **Not for everyone.** Covenda is deliberately *not* a whole-student-body platform. It's
  for **highly motivated, high-achieving, professor/club-vetted** students. Scarcity and
  vetting are the product.
- **Handshake's moat vs. its weakness.** Handshake owns distribution via deep university
  partnerships (auto-funnels entire student bodies; freemium SaaS where 950k+ employers
  use a free tier and enterprises pay ~$12k–$100k+ for advanced candidate segmentation).
  *We do not fight them on distribution.* We attack the thing they're weak on: **quality
  and signal.** Their scale is now their liability — AI-mass-applied volume poisons the
  candidate pool for employers and buries good students among millions.
- **For students:** a way to *differentiate* from the AI-spam masses. Covenda is where a
  serious student's real signal (a vouch, a demonstrated skill, actual work) counts —
  instead of being one of a thousand identical AI-written applications.
- **For companies:** the quality service Handshake doesn't provide — a short list of
  pre-vetted, skills-verified people, not a résumé pile to sift.

## THE TWO AUDIENCES — key messages

**Students (the motivated minority):**
- "Stop competing on volume you can't win." Mass AI applications are a race to zero;
  Covenda is the opposite bet — be vouched-for and demonstrably capable, and be *seen*.
- The application is **signal, not spam**: a professor/club referral, a short pre-recorded
  video intro (HireVue-style), work samples (GitHub, research papers), and a rich,
  reviewed batch application for specialized verticals.
- The smartest students increasingly want **startups**, not the finance-internship
  treadmill — and startup access today runs on personal connections + small YC-style
  cohorts. Covenda universalizes that access.

**Companies (seed/small firms + startups):**
- "Skills over résumés." Lead with the NACE finding that a large majority of employers
  now hire skills-first — the degree matters less than demonstrated ability, which is
  exactly what Covenda surfaces. *(Grounding: NACE Job Outlook 2026 "% using skills-based
  hiring" — verify before publishing.)*
- "AI-poisoned résumés are a filtering tax." Covenda removes it: every candidate is
  referral-backed and reviewed, so you evaluate a handful of real people, not a spam pile.
- Reduced burden + shifted risk (ties into the existing pay-on-acceptance / packet model).

## CONCRETE WEBSITE CHANGES (what to build)
1. **A "Why now / the problem" narrative** — the four forces above, on the shared story
   section, honestly (published research, not Covenda claims).
2. **Student hero: reframe around differentiation** — "be the signal, not the spam."
   Make the rich-application features (referral, video intro, work samples, batch) the
   visible proof that Covenda is quality-first.
3. **Company hero: lead with skills-based hiring + the anti-spam promise** (builds on the
   new two-segment "delegate work / find elite talent" hero).
4. **An explicit "Covenda vs. the job-board pile" contrast** — vouched + reviewed short
   list vs. thousand-deep AI-spam applicant stack. (Contrast, never naming a competitor
   disparagingly; frame as "open marketplaces" vs. "curated.")
5. **Vetting/scarcity made tangible** — say plainly that Covenda is selective and
   professor/club-vouched; that exclusivity is the value, not a limitation.

## OPEN QUESTIONS TO RESOLVE (don't guess — confirm with the founder)
- **Finance tension.** The ideation says Covenda wants *no exposure to finance
  internships* — yet the current GTM wedge is *accounting / finance-ops work for small
  firms*. These may be different (recruiting-for-IB vs. doing finance-ops project work),
  but the site must not send mixed signals. Clarify the boundary.
- **Students vs. work.** Is the near-term product "get scoped project work done" (the
  packet/marketplace we've built) or "get hired as an intern" (a recruiting layer)? The
  narrative above spans both; the site should be explicit about which is live now.

## GUARDRAILS
- **Nothing fabricated.** No stat ships until it's traced to a real, citable source; the
  section keeps the existing "these cite published research, not Covenda results" note.
- **Anti-spam is the whole point** — never add features that reward volume or make
  AI-generated mass applications easier.
- **Honest stage** — pilot framing; no invented partnerships, counts, or outcomes.
