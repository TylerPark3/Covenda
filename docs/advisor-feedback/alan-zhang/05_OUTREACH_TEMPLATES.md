# Hiring-Manager Outreach Templates

## Cold email

**Subject:** Columbia student researching early technical talent

Hi [Name],

I’m a Columbia Engineering student building Covenda, a company focused on helping strong early-career candidates demonstrate what they can actually do beyond a resume.

I’m researching one narrow question:

**What evidence actually predicts whether an undergraduate software engineer will become useful on a demanding technical team?**

I’m not asking you to buy anything or review candidates. I’m trying to learn directly from engineers and managers who have supervised interns rather than relying only on recruiting teams.

I would especially value your perspective on what separates the strongest interns, which capabilities are hard to identify from resumes, what kind of longer-horizon work product would impress your team, and where strong candidates are overlooked.

Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation?

Best,  
Tyler Park  
Co-founder, Covenda  
Columbia Engineering
