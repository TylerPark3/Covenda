Finance evidence

Add work you have done.

A link is what lets this count as more than self-reported. Without one it still appears, marked as listed rather than shown. The as-of date matters because a call is only judgeable against what was knowable when you made it.

The downside is the field that separates a view from a summary. A pitch without one reads as marketing, and it is the first thing a reviewer asks about.

Coursework

Add a course you have taken.

A course is recorded as self-reported and is not used for matching. It sets what a company can fairly ask you about, and Covenda turns it into a list of work that would actually prove it. No grade is stored, because a transcript line cannot tell a top mark from a pass.
